About the role

As a Junior Consultant you support international consulting projects in project coordination, planning, analysis, documentation and implementation across the full project lifecycle.

Your responsibilities

  • Support project coordination, planning and task management
  • Assist with project management, change management and organizational development
  • Support process, stakeholder, risk and time management
  • Prepare and document meetings and workshops
  • Support workshop facilitation and follow-up
  • Collaborate on project analysis, effort estimation and OKR tracking
  • Create presentations, reports and project documents
  • Work closely with international project teams and stakeholders

Your profile

  • Good IT understanding and a strong analytical mindset
  • Structured, precise working style with attention to detail
  • Fluency in German and English is required; additional languages are a plus
  • Basic understanding of project management, planning and evaluation
  • Foundational knowledge of change management, organizational development and stakeholder communication
  • Understanding of meeting and workshop management, moderation and follow-up
  • Basic understanding of OKRs and effort estimation
  • Confident with MS Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Project and Visio
  • Strong communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills
  • Open to feedback and eager to learn
  • Intercultural awareness and enjoyment of working in international teams

What we offer

  • The opportunity to grow your career in an international consulting environment
  • Hands-on exposure to a range of projects, clients and industries
  • An environment focused on learning, feedback and continuous development
  • Work with international teams on transformation projects that make a real impact
  • Flat hierarchies and an open company culture
  • Annual team retreats (Barcelona, Paris, Lisbon, Prague and others)
  • Flexible working hours and the option to work remotely within the EU
  • Regular team events and benefits
